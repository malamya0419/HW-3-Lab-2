In this lab, I used the Household Pulse Survey dataset to examine how partnering rates differ by age, educational attainment, and state of residency (New York vs. New Jersey). Age was the most significant factor in partnering across all educational levels, with rates gradually increasing from the 25–34 age group to the 55–65 age group.  A person's decision to get married or have a relationship can be influenced by their level of education. Compared to those with a lower degree of education, people with more years of education typically marry later but eventually have higher and more stable partnering rates. Additionally, throughout a wide range of educational degrees, New Jersey typically has somewhat higher partnering rates than New York.
